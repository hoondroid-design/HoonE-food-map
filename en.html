"What to Eat in Gangneung...?"

Intro.

Started living in Seoul around 2017. Ever since being tagged as a "Gangneung native," I’ve been asked "Where should I eat in Gangneung?" hundreds of times. Gathering memories of cafes, restaurants, and local spots I frequented during my 25+ years in Gangneung, I shared them via Google Sheets—and now we’ve jumped onto the AI wave to build this map. Locals eat home-cooked meals when visiting family; I’ve only had Jjamppong Sundubu once in my entire life. We just go to our usual places. So I don't really know much about trending Instagram/Reels hotspots either—you probably know them better than I do! Everyone's taste is different, so please judge for yourself. Most spots here have been around for over 10 years, though flavors or vibes might shift if owners change. Ah, there are ZERO sponsored reviews here. Visited everywhere in person. (Though I'd love to make big bucks from ads someday!)
